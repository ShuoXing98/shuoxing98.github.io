abstract: >-
  We study the approximation capability of the orthogonal super greedy algorithm (OSGA) with respect to $\mu$-coherent dictionaries in Hilbert spaces, establishing Lebesgue-type inequalities which show that OSGA provides an almost optimal approximation. We also improve the asymptotic constant in the Lebesgue-type inequality of OGA.
