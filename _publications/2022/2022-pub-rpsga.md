abstract: >-
  We propose the Weak Rescaled Pure Super Greedy Algorithm (WRPSGA) for approximation with respect to a dictionary in Hilbert space and show that its convergence rate on the closure of the convex hull of a $\mu$-coherent dictionary is optimal. We further design the Rescaled Pure Super Greedy Learning Algorithm (RPSGLA) for kernel-based supervised learning, with a convergence rate arbitrarily close to the best rate $O(m^{-1})$.
