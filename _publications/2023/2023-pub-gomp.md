abstract: >-
  We propose the Group Orthogonal Matching Pursuit (GOMP) algorithm to recover group sparse signals from noisy measurements. Under the group restricted isometry property, we prove the instance optimality and robustness of GOMP, and simulations show that it significantly outperforms Basis Pursuit in both scalability and solution quality.
