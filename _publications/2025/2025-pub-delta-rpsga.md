abstract: >-
  We propose the $\delta$-Rescaled Pure Super Greedy Algorithm ($\delta$-RPSGA) for approximation with respect to a dictionary in a Hilbert space and show that its convergence rate on the closure of the convex hull of a Riesz dictionary is optimal. We further design the $\delta$-RPSGLA for kernel-based regression and derive convergence rates arbitrarily close to the best rate for infinitely smooth kernels.
