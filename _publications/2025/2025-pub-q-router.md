abstract: >-
  We propose Q-Router, an agentic framework for universal video quality assessment with a multi-tier model routing system, which employs vision-language models as real-time routers that dynamically reason over and ensemble the most appropriate expert models conditioned on the input video semantics. Q-Router matches or surpasses state-of-the-art VQA models across UGC, short-form, and AI-generated video benchmarks while substantially improving generalization and interpretability, and its spatiotemporal artifact localization shows potential as a reward function for post-training video generation models.
