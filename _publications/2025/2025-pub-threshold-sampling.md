abstract: >-
  We address submodular maximization under bandit feedback, where the objective can only be accessed through noisy queries, and focus on the pure-exploration setting of identifying a high-quality solution set with as few queries as possible. We propose Confident Sample (CS), an efficient adaptive sampling subroutine that yields approximation guarantees arbitrarily close to the standard value oracle setting for monotone submodular maximization under cardinality and matroid constraints, as well as unconstrained non-monotone submodular maximization.
