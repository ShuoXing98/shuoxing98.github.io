abstract: >-
  This paper presents the ISRGC-Q Challenge, built upon the Image Super-Resolution Generated Content Quality Assessment (ISRGen-QA) dataset and organized as part of the VQualA Competition at the ICCV 2025 Workshops. Unlike existing SR-IQA datasets, ISRGen-QA emphasizes SR images generated by the latest generative approaches, including GANs and diffusion models.
