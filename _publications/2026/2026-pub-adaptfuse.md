abstract: >-
  We propose AdaptFuse, a training-free framework that externalizes probabilistic computation from the LLM: a symbolic module maintains a Bayesian posterior over a discrete hypothesis set, while a frozen LLM contributes semantic reasoning, and the two signals are combined through entropy-adaptive fusion. Across flight recommendation, hotel recommendation, and web shopping, AdaptFuse consistently outperforms both prompting baselines and fine-tuned Bayesian Teaching models, without storing or training on sensitive user data.
