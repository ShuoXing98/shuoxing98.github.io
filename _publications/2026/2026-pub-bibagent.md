abstract: >-
  We introduce BibAgent, a scalable, end-to-end agentic framework for automated citation verification that integrates retrieval, reasoning, and adaptive evidence aggregation, with a novel Evidence Committee mechanism that infers the validity of citations to paywalled sources via downstream citation consensus. We also contribute a 5-category Miscitation Taxonomy and MisciteBench, a cross-disciplinary benchmark of 6,350 miscitation samples spanning 254 fields.
