abstract: >-
  We introduce CAPS (Cascaded Adaptive Pairwise Selection), an inference-only framework for parallel reasoning that allocates verifier compute non-uniformly along two axes: an evidence axis that adapts how much of each candidate the judge sees, and a distribution axis that adapts how comparisons are spread across the pool. On four self-verifying models and five code and math benchmarks, CAPS outperforms the leading pairwise verifier on 14 of 20 suites while using 25.4% of its verifier-token budget on code.
