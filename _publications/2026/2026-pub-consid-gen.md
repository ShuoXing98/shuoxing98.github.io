abstract: >-
  We address identity preservation under changing viewpoints in image-to-video generation from both data and model perspectives. We curate ConsIDVid, a large-scale object-centric dataset with the ConsIDVid-Bench evaluation framework for multi-view consistency, and propose ConsID-Gen, a view-assisted I2V framework that augments the first frame with unposed auxiliary views and fuses semantic and structural cues via a dual-stream visual-geometric encoder. ConsID-Gen surpasses leading video generation models like Wan2.1 and HunyuanVideo, delivering superior identity fidelity and temporal coherence.
