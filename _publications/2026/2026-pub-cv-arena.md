abstract: >-
  We introduce CV-Arena, an open benchmark for instructional computer vision problem solving, containing 12K high-resolution real-image instruction pairs spanning 16 instruction-based visual task types. To evaluate models at scale while preserving human fidelity, we propose Active Elo, a human-AI collaborative preference protocol that uses CV-Judge, a multi-dimensional VLM evaluator, to resolve clear comparisons and routes close, high-quality comparisons to expert raters. Our evaluation of 21 systems reveals persistent gaps in instruction adherence, physical reasoning, structural control, and fine-grained detail preservation.
