abstract: >-
  Once the evaluator is frozen, a sufficiently strong search process can learn to win the exam without learning the mechanism the task was meant to reveal. We introduce DASES, a falsification-driven framework in which an Innovator, an Abyss Falsifier, and a Mechanistic Causal Extractor co-evolve executable scientific artifacts and admissible counterexample environments. In a controlled loss-discovery problem, DASES discovers FNG-CE, a loss that transfers beyond the discovery environment and consistently outperforms CE and CE+L2 across standard benchmarks, including ImageNet.
