abstract: >-
  We investigate whether Kolmogorov–Arnold Networks (KANs) can serve as effective modeling components for long-term time series forecasting, and propose KANMixer, a compact KAN-centered architecture consisting of a multi-scale pooling frontend, a KAN-based temporal mixing backbone, and prediction heads. Across 28 benchmark-horizon settings against nine baselines, KANMixer achieves the best MSE in 16 settings, and ablations reveal that design choices that benefit MLPs, such as decomposition priors, can degrade KANs.
