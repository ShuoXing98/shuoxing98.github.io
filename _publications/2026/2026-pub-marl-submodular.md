abstract: >-
  We study cooperative multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL) where the joint reward exhibits submodularity, capturing diminishing marginal returns when adding agents to a team, and provide the first formal framework for this setting. For known dynamics, our greedy policy optimization achieves a $1/2$-approximation with polynomial complexity in the number of agents; for unknown dynamics, we propose a UCB-based learning algorithm achieving a $1/2$-regret of $O(H^2KS\sqrt{AT})$.
