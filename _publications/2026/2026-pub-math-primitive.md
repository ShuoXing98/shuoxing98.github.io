abstract: >-
  We take a first step toward systematically studying mathematical understanding in LLMs, from diagnosing its distinct capabilities to leveraging these findings to improve post-training. We introduce the notion of Mathematical Primitive to probe structural mathematical understanding and propose Prim, a benchmark that evaluates mathematical reasoning along four dimensions: Discovery, Generation, Digestion, and Execution. Our diagnosis shows that solution accuracy masks distinct capability profiles, primitives unlock substantial latent execution capacity, and Discovery is the dominant bottleneck. Building on these findings, we introduce Absorb, a primitive-privileged self-distillation framework that consistently improves mathematical reasoning across model scales and challenging benchmarks.
