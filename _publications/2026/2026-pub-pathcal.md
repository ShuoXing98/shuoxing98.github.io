abstract: >-
  Through type-wise suppression and fixed-prefix intervention, we reveal that reflection markers in long chain-of-thought (e.g., "wait", "but", "alternatively") differ not only in their functional roles but also in when they exert the greatest influence. Motivated by these findings, we introduce PathCal, a training-free decoding controller that calibrates reasoning paths by distinguishing marker types and intervening only at locally uncertain states. Experiments across six reasoning benchmarks show that PathCal improves or preserves accuracy while reducing generation length, without external verifiers or additional sampling.
