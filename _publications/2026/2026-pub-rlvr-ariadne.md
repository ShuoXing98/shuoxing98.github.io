abstract: >-
  We introduce Ariadne, a controlled framework based on synthetic maze navigation where reasoning difficulty is precisely regulated, to examine the impact of Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) on the capability boundaries of Vision-Language Models. We show that RLVR extends the spatial reasoning boundary, succeeding on problems where the base model consistently attains 0% accuracy despite increasing pass@k budgets, and that the gains transfer zero-shot to real-world navigation benchmarks (MapBench and ReasonMap).
