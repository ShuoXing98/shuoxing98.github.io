abstract: >-
  We propose TimePre, a simple framework that unifies the efficiency of MLP-based models with the distributional flexibility of Multiple Choice Learning (MCL) for probabilistic time-series forecasting. Its core, Stabilized Instance Normalization (SIN), corrects channel-wise statistical shifts to resolve catastrophic hypothesis collapse. TimePre achieves state-of-the-art accuracy on six benchmarks with inference speeds orders of magnitude faster than sampling-based models.
