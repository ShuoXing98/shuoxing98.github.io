abstract: >-
  We propose Traversal-as-Policy: distill sandboxed agent execution logs into a single executable Gated Behavior Tree (GBT) and treat tree traversal, rather than unconstrained generation, as the control policy, with safety gates mined from unsafe traces and risk-aware recovery when the agent stalls. Across 15+ software, web, reasoning, and safety benchmarks, GBT improves success while driving violations toward zero and reducing cost; on SWE-bench Verified, it raises success from 34.6% to 73.6% and reduces violations from 2.8% to 0.2%.
