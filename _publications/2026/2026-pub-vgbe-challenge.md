abstract: >-
  This paper presents an overview of the VGBE 2026 Challenge on Image-to-Video Consistent Generation, which addresses temporal distortion and appearance drift in generative video. Given a single reference image and a text prompt, participants generated video sequences that adhere to text instructions while maintaining visual identity, geometric coherence, and high perceptual quality, evaluated with a composite protocol of objective metrics and subjective human assessments.
